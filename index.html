Research

  • Fusion near the barrier

    Fusion excitation functions and barrier distributions for heavy-ion systems at energies below and near the Coulomb barrier.

  • Fission and quasifission

    Compound-nucleus formation probabilities, nuclear dissipation and the onset of quasifission in heavy systems.

  • Evaporation residues

    Residue cross-section measurements with recoil separators for nuclei in the rare-earth and A ≈ 200 regions.

  • Radioactive ion beams

    Reactions of halo and neutron-rich beams — 9Li, 11Li, 132Sn — with heavy targets.

  • Multinucleon transfer

    Transfer channels as a route to neutron-rich nuclei, studied with magnetic spectrometers.

  • Instrumentation

    Magnetic spectrometers, recoil separators, gas detectors and thin isotopic target fabrication.
